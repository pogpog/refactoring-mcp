# DesignPatterns

![image](#) ![image](#) ![image](#) ![image](#) ![image](#) ![image](#) ![image](#) 
 **Design patterns** are typical solutions to common problems
in software design. Each pattern is like a blueprint
that you can customize to solve a particular
design problem in your code. [What's a design pattern?](Design%20Patterns/What%20Is%20Pattern.md) 
### Catalog of patterns
 List of 22 classic design patterns,
grouped by their intent. [Look inside the catalog »](Design%20Patterns/Catalog.md) 
### Benefits of patterns
 Patterns are a toolkit of solutions to common
problems in software design. They define
a common language that helps your team
communicate more efficiently. [More about the benefits »](Design%20Patterns/Why%20Learn%20Patterns.md) 
### Classification
 Design patterns differ by their complexity, level of
detail and scale of applicability. In addition,
they can be categorized by their intent
and divided into three groups. [More about the categories »](Design%20Patterns/Classification.md) 
### History of patterns
 Who invented patterns and when?
Can you use patterns outside software
development? How do you do that? [More about the history »](Design%20Patterns/History.md) 
### Criticism of patterns
 Are patterns as good as advertised?
Is it always possible to use them?
Can patterns sometimes be harmful? [More about the criticism »](Design%20Patterns/Criticism.md)
